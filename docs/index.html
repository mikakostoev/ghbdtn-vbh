Keyboard layout auto-switcher for macOS. Free, open source, and nothing phones home.

Install

brew tap mikakostoev/ghbdtn-vbh https://github.com/mikakostoev/ghbdtn-vbh && brew install --cask --no-quarantine ghbdtn-vbh

Or download the zip and drag ghbdtn vbh.app into Applications. macOS 13 or newer, Apple silicon and Intel.

On first launch macOS blocks the app: click “Open Anyway” in System Settings → Privacy & Security, then allow it under Accessibility. The app opens a window that walks you through it. There is no paid Apple certificate behind the build, so the same permission is asked again after an update.

Upgrading from LayoutSwitcher: remove the old app first — brew uninstall --cask layoutswitcher --zap, or drag LayoutSwitcher.app to the Trash — otherwise both switchers stay running and fight over the same keystrokes. Turn “Open at login” back on in the new app; that setting doesn't carry over.

What it does

  • Fixes the word on the spot: ghbdtn → «привет», руддщ → «hello». Any layout macOS knows, not only Russian and English.
  • Knows slang and names the system dictionary doesn't, from letter statistics: ltdjgcjd → «девопсов».
  • Copes with Caps Lock, abbreviations, identifiers like getUserName, numbers and stretched words.
  • Double Option converts the last word or your selection by hand.
  • Exceptions per word, per app and per site.

Why it can be trusted with your keyboard

  • No network, none. No updates, statistics, licence checks or crash reports. One grep over the source finds no networking library.
  • The code is short and open. About a thousand lines of Swift, readable in an evening.
  • Nothing you type is kept. Memory holds the word you are typing and at most three short words before it. Never written to disk.
  • Passwords are off limits. In a secure field the app does not look at the keys at all.
  • Three plain text files in Application Support: your exceptions, learned words and excluded sites. All visible in Settings.

Languages

Works like Russian and English do: Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Danish, Norwegian, Swedish, Finnish, Icelandic, Czech, Polish, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Greek, Turkish, Lithuanian, Hebrew, Arabic. Only Russian, English, Ukrainian and German are actually checked on texts; the others use the same mechanism.

Weaker without a system dictionary (Croatian, Slovak, Estonian, Macedonian, Albanian, Serbian, Farsi, Georgian) and for layouts that share letters with English. Doesn't work for input methods (Chinese, Japanese, Korean), scripts without spaces, and dead-key layouts. The honest list is in the README.
